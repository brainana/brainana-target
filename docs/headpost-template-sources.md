# Headpost design sources reviewed for BrainanaTarget

Reviewed 20 October 2026. These entries identify designs and verified measurements; they do not imply that a complete CAD model is bundled. Manufacturer photos are not used to infer missing dimensions. The existing circular preview remains a custom planning envelope. A manufacturer or fitted headpost uses its imported STL.

| Resource | What it supports in the app | Model availability / limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gray Matter Research headposts](https://www.graymatter-research.com/head-posts) | Separate DAHP-1, DAHP-2 and PEEK design references. | The supplied product page shows design families, rather than a complete dimension set or downloadable model used by this app. The K5 listing contains insufficient detail for a usable template and is not added as an empty preset. |
| [Thomas primate chairs](https://www.thomasrecording.com/np-primate-products-primate-chairs) | Related chair/head-holder compatibility link, alongside the existing PPS-pin reference. | This page describes chair-mounted single-post and three-point holders. Its chair dimensions do not define an implanted post. No new implant dimensions are inferred from it. |
| [Overton et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5814707/) | DAHP-2 foot thickness 1.39 mm; reported screw opening 3 mm and countersink opening 5 mm. These appear with their source. | The illustrated implant has feet bent and shortened for an individual skull. Those few measurements cannot reconstruct its complete shape. No pitch is inferred from the reported gaps between openings. |
| [Adams et al. 2007](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.00102.2007?doi=10.1152%2Fjn.00102.2007) | Original Mark II reference, explicitly dated 2007, with 1 mm footplate. | Publisher indexing lists supplementary AutoCAD drawing `ucsf2008-002a.dwg`. It was identified, but not retrieved or converted into a bundled STL. The app imports STL, not DWG. |
| [Adams et al. 2011, PMC3174814](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3174814/) | Separate updated headpost reference with 1.5 mm footplate. | Primarily a chamber paper. It also reports this headpost change and other minor revisions. The app does not silently thicken the 2007 design or assume the remaining geometry is identical. |
| [Moore et al. 2026, PMC13603819](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13603819/) | Related protective-cap resource, outside the headpost selector. | A removable cranial cap attached to a headpost is an accessory, not a replacement implant. Article identity and scope were confirmed through [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42791685/); an [author preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.0381) was also reviewed. No verified downloadable CAD/STL was obtained. |
| [Crist Instrument](https://www.cristinstrument.com/) | A separate reference for an actual measured/manufacturer-supplied Crist headpost, retaining its part name and imported STL. | The supplied homepage identifies the implant/head-holder product family. Complete current part dimensions were not retrieved. Historical parts described in the Adams paper are not represented as current manufacturer specifications. |

The existing ESI modular reference remains available. Selection and dimensions survive tab changes. New reference identifiers are accepted by project validation and round-trip in portable projects. No saved headpost geometry, fit criterion, or placement constraint is changed merely by selecting one of these literature/product references.
